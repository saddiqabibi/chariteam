Our Purpose, Outlook & Principles

Our Mission

To promote understanding of education, clean water, and healthcare issues affecting children in underprivileged communities, by providing truthful, well-researched information.

Our Vision

A society where individuals are educated on genuine problems, and where understanding fosters impactful actions and assistance for those in greatest need.

Our Values

Integrity, openness, and a dedication to truth. We don’t overstate. We avoid making misleading commitments. We just convey what we understand.
