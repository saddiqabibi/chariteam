About Us

We Help People In Need Around The World

"We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive. Our mission is to make that possible."

John Anderson, Founder

ChariTeam is a nonprofit organization dedicated to transforming lives through education, clean water, and healthcare. Since 2010, we've helped over 50,000 children across 15 countries. Join us in our mission to create a better world.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

You can donate through our website by clicking the "Donate Now" button. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Every donation goes directly to our programs helping children and communities in need.

85% of your donation directly funds our programs including education, clean water, and healthcare. 10% goes to operational costs and 5% to administrative expenses. We are transparent about our finances.

Yes, ChariTeam is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law. You will receive a receipt for your records.

We welcome volunteers! You can sign up on our website or contact us directly. We have opportunities both locally and internationally in education, healthcare, and community development programs.

We operate in 15 countries across Africa, Asia, and South America. Our programs focus on communities with the greatest need for education, clean water, and healthcare services.
