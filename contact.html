How We Can Help

What You Can Contact Us About

To help us respond faster, please choose the right subject when contacting us. Here's a guide on what we can assist with.

General Questions

Have a question about our articles, our platform, or how we work? We're happy to answer. We try to provide clear, helpful responses to every query.

Corrections & Feedback

Accuracy matters to us. If you find an error in any of our articles, please let us know. We review all corrections carefully and update our content when needed.

Guest Contributions

If you have expertise in education, clean water, or healthcare, we welcome guest articles. Please include a brief outline of your idea with your message.

Partnerships

We're open to collaborating with organizations, researchers, and writers who share our mission. Reach out with details about your organization and proposal.

Content Sharing

Want to share or republish our content? Contact us first so we can discuss proper attribution and permissions. We generally allow sharing with credit.

Suggest A Topic

Have an idea for an article? We love hearing from our readers. Suggest a topic that you think deserves more attention, and we'll consider it for our editorial calendar.

Please Note

What We Cannot Help With

To be transparent, there are some things we cannot assist with. This helps us focus our limited resources on what we do best.

Donation Collection

We do not collect or process donations. If you wish to donate, please support a registered charity organization directly.

Financial Assistance

We cannot provide financial aid or direct assistance to individuals. We are a content platform, not a charity.

Medical or Legal Advice

We share information, but we cannot provide professional medical or legal advice. Please consult qualified professionals for such matters.

Urgent Requests

We are a small team and cannot handle urgent or emergency requests. For emergencies, please contact local authorities or emergency services.
