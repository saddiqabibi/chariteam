Frequently Asked Questions

You can donate through our website by clicking the "Donate Now" button. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Every donation goes directly to our programs.

85% of your donation directly funds our programs including education, clean water, and healthcare. 10% goes to operational costs and 5% to administrative expenses.

We welcome volunteers! You can sign up through our contact form or call us directly. We have opportunities both locally and internationally.

We operate in 15 countries across Africa, Asia, and South America. Our programs focus on communities with the greatest need.

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly during business hours.
