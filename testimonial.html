Our Commitment

Transparency, Accuracy, and Integrity

At ChariTeam, we believe that trust is earned through honesty and consistency. This page explains how we create our content, how we handle corrections, and what you can expect from us.

Editorial Standards

How We Create Our Content

Research First

Every article begins with research. We gather information from credible sources including UNICEF, WHO, UNESCO, academic journals, and verified news outlets. We do not publish unverified claims.

Original Writing

All our articles are written from scratch. We do not copy, spin, or auto-generate content. Our writers craft each piece with care, using clear and accessible language.

Review Process

Before publication, every article goes through a review process. We check facts, verify sources, and ensure the content meets our quality standards.

Our Promise

What You Can Expect From Us

No Fake Claims

We do not exaggerate numbers, invent statistics, or make false promises. If we don't know something, we say so.

Cited Sources

Wherever possible, we link to our sources so you can verify the information yourself. We believe in transparency, not blind trust.

Regular Updates

We update our articles when new information becomes available. Accuracy is an ongoing commitment, not a one-time task.

Open to Feedback

If you find an error or have a suggestion, we want to hear from you. We take all feedback seriously and act on it.

Privacy Respect

We respect your privacy. We don't sell your data, and we don't spam your inbox. See our Privacy Policy for details.

No Hidden Agendas

We are not affiliated with any charity. We don't collect donations. Our only goal is to inform and raise awareness.

Corrections

How We Handle Mistakes

We are human, and we make mistakes. What matters is how we handle them. Here's our corrections policy:

1
Report the Error

If you find an error, contact us with the article link and details.

2
We Verify

Our team reviews the claim and checks the original sources.

3
We Correct

If an error is confirmed, we update the article and note the correction.

4
We Notify

For significant corrections, we notify readers through our newsletter.
